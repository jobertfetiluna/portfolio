About me

Get to know my journey and education.

I’m a BSIT student at Cebu Eastern College, and I’ve been building websites since my 1st year. I like turning an idea into a page that people can open and use, and I learn best by trying things and seeing what happens.

When I get stuck, I go one step at a time: check what I wrote, test it, and look for the line that causes the problem. I’m not afraid to ask for help, and I enjoy working with my classmates on group tasks.

I use VS Code and Antigravity to help me write code, and I try to understand what it does before I use it. I put my websites online with Vercel, and this portfolio is one of them.
